# Data use and attribution

The underlying records are CDC/NCHS public-use natality data. Conversion to Parquet does not remove their conditions or confer new rights over the source. Use these records for statistical analysis and reporting only. Do not attempt to identify people or establishments, link to individually identifiable records, or investigate re-identification or disclosure-protection methods. The authoritative conditions are the [NCHS Data User Agreement](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html).

The [NVSS release policy](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nvss/dvs_data_release.htm) describes the public-use scope. Original files are available without charge from NCHS. A locally computed checksum identifies the acquired copy; it is not an authenticated publisher checksum.

Source: CDC/NCHS. The conversions are an independent research resource and are not endorsed by CDC, HHS or the U.S. Government. [CDC material-use policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html).

The package's software license does not replace the source-data conditions. Cite the source data, data years and conversion release separately. Do not describe source-preserving access as independently validated clinical harmonization.
